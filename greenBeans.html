Green Beans Almondine

Ingredients

  • About 1 pound of fresh green beans
  • 2 tbsp salted butter
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup of raw almonds
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • (Optional) Pinch of baking soda

Steps

  1. Wash the green beans
  2. Fill a pot, big enough to fit all of your green beans, with water and bring to a boil
  3. (Optional) If you want the green beans to stay as vibrant as possible, add the pinch of baking soda so they can keep their color
  4. Throw the green beans into the water and let them boil for about 3 minutes until al dente
  5. While the green beans are cooking, mince your garlic and roughly chop your almonds up
  6. After 3 minutes, turn the stove off and put your green beans into a strainer and run cold water on them so that they can stop cooking
  7. Put the olive oil and half of the butter into a pan on medium heat and toast the almonds for about 3 minutes
  8. Turn the heat down to medium-low and mix in the green beans and garlic
  9. After about a minute of stirring, add in the salt, pepper, and the rest of the butter and keep stirring for about 2-3 more minutes
  10. Enjoy!
