Cornbread

Ingredients

  • 1 cup corn meal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 6 tbsp melted unsalted butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F
  2. Butter a 9 inch round baking pan with 1 tbsp of your melted butter
  3. In a large bowl, mix the cornmeal, flour, sugar, salt, and baking powder until thoroughly combined
  4. Once the dry ingredients are combined, add in your milk, egg, and the rest of the melted butter. Mix until well combined, but be sure not to overmix it.
  5. Pour the batter into the pan and put in oven for 20-25 minutes
  6. After 20 minutes check on the cornbread by sticking a wooden toothpick into the middle. If it comes out clean, you can take the bread out of the oven, but if it still needs to cook more, put it back for a few minutes until cooked all the way through
  7. Onced cooked you are free to enjoy!
