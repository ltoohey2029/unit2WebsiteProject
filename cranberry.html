Cranberry Sauce

Ingredients

  • 12 oz of fresh cranberries
  • 1 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • (Optional) 1 peeled and cored Granny Smith apple
  • (Optional) 1 orange
  • (Optional) 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Throw the cranberries, sugar, water, cinnamon, and nutmeg into a sauce pan and put on stove at medium heat for 5 minutes, making sure to stir occasionally
  2. If you decide to add the optional fruits, dice up the apple and zest and juice the orange and lemon
  3. Once the 5 minutes is up, take out the cinnamon stick and add the apple and the lemon and orange zest and juice
  4. Let the mixture cook for another 15 minutes on medium-low heat, stirring occasionally
  5. After the 15 minutes, your sauce is done. If you don't like a chunky texture for your cranberry sauce, you can blend it some
  6. After 3 minutes, turn the stove off and put your green beans into a strainer and run cold water on them so that they can stop cooking
  7. Put the olive oil and half of the butter into a pan on medium heat and toast the almonds for about 3 minutes
  8. Turn the heat down to medium-low and mix in the green beans and garlic
  9. After about a minute of stirring, add in the salt, pepper, and the rest of the butter and keep stirring for about 2-3 more minutes
  10. Enjoy!
