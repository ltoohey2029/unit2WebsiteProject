Easy Side Dish Recipes for the Thanksgiving!

Here are three fun and easy recipes by Laura Prepon to complete your holiday meals!

    Green Beans Almondine
    Corn Bread
    Cranberry Sauce


These recipes were made by actor, director, and author Laura Prepon, who has been cooking since she was a child and posts weekly cooking videos. She has learned all of her cooking skills from her mother, who was a gourmet chef. She is most famous for starring in "Orange is the New Black" and "That '70s Show".
